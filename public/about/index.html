About Sandia Purpose Foundation

Practical support.
Lasting purpose.

A New Mexico charitable organization focused on individuals and families facing hardship.

Who we are

Sandia Purpose Foundation is organized for charitable and educational purposes. Its mission is to help people facing hardship find practical support, recovery resources, and paths to greater stability.

The Foundation’s charitable purpose is broad. Its bylaws allow basic-needs assistance, community outreach, recovery support and peer mentorship, housing assistance, workforce development, veteran assistance, education, and connections to treatment and community resources.

Where we are today

We are establishing the Foundation and raising donations and capital funding to make a meaningful impact. Our present fundraising priorities are our first employee, office space, supplies, and outreach.

These priorities describe the capacity we are raising funds to establish. They do not represent a list of staffed programs or guaranteed assistance. Contact the Foundation directly to discuss a request, a contribution, or an opportunity to help.

A separate charitable organization

Sandia Purpose Foundation has its own nonprofit purpose and governance. Sandia Purpose Recovery Services LLC and Sandia Purpose Productions are separate from the Foundation and have separate websites.

Charitable gifts through this site support Sandia Purpose Foundation. A Foundation donation does not purchase housing services or freelance videography.
