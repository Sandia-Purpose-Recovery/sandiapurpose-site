Our mission

Our mission is to help people in New Mexico build lasting recovery through peer mentorship, housing assistance, education, and pathways to work. We aim to support veterans and connect people to treatment and community resources.

Sandia Purpose Foundation is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity. EIN: 42-4902495.

What is Sandia Purpose Foundation?

Sandia Purpose Foundation is a nonprofit foundation focused on lasting recovery in New Mexico. Its mission includes peer mentorship, housing assistance, education, and pathways to work.

How can I support the Foundation?

You can give through PayPal Giving Fund or contact the Foundation about your time, skills, resources, or ideas.

Explore ways to get involved
Where do donations go?

The donation links on this page recommend Sandia Purpose Foundation as the benefiting charity. PayPal Giving Fund handles the donation and provides your receipt under its donation policies.

Where do I ask about housing or make a referral?

Housing, intake, and referral inquiries belong with Sandia Purpose Recovery Services LLC, which is separate from the Foundation.
