Sandia Purpose Foundation

Our mission

◆

Our mission is to help people in New Mexico build lasting recovery through peer mentorship, housing assistance, education, and pathways to work. We aim to support veterans and connect people to treatment and community resources.

Sandia Purpose Foundation is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity.

Ask us a question ↗
How do I request recovery housing?

Review the housing information and book an intake call with Sandia Purpose Recovery Services. The call is a starting point to discuss your needs and the next steps.

Find housing support →
Can I refer someone else?

Yes. Families, treatment and discharge teams, case managers, and community partners can request an intake call. Let us know that you’re making a referral and your relationship to the person.

See referral next steps →
Does booking an intake call guarantee a place?

No. Placement depends on screening, program fit, safety, and availability. The intake conversation helps establish what comes next.

Are the foundation and recovery housing the same organization?

Sandia Purpose Foundation and Sandia Purpose Recovery Services LLC are separate organizations. This page introduces the foundation’s mission. Housing and intake links take you to Sandia Purpose Recovery Services.

How can I support the foundation?

Get in touch about your time, skills, resources, or ideas. We can discuss ways to support the foundation’s mission and how you’d like to be involved.

Find a way to help →
What information should I share when I reach out?

Start with your name, how to reach you, and a brief description of what you need or how you’d like to help. Please keep detailed medical history out of email and booking notes.
