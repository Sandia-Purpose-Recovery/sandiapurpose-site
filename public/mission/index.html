Our mission & charitable purpose

Meet hardship
with practical support.

To help individuals and families facing hardship find practical support, recovery resources, and paths to greater stability.

Start with the person

Hardship rarely fits into a single category. Immediate needs, recovery, education, work, and family stability can overlap. The Foundation’s mission recognizes that people may need both practical help and a clearer path to the next step.

Its charitable purpose brings those needs into one broad framework: meet immediate needs while helping people move toward safer, healthier, and more stable lives.

The purpose guiding our development

Basic needs and outreach
The Foundation’s bylaws include food, water, and other basic-needs assistance, together with community outreach.
Recovery and connections
Its purpose includes recovery support, peer mentorship, and connections to treatment providers and community resources.
Stability and opportunity
Housing assistance, education, workforce development, and veteran assistance are also within the Foundation’s charitable purpose.

Our current activity: establishing the Foundation

We are seeking donations and capital funding for the people, space, supplies, and outreach capacity needed to make a meaningful impact. The areas above describe our charitable purpose and the work it allows us to develop.

For current opportunities to contribute, or to ask what support may be available, contact the Foundation. A direct conversation lets us give an accurate answer about the stage of our work and the request you have in mind.
