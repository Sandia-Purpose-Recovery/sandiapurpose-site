Help establish the Foundation

Our current work is establishing Sandia Purpose Foundation and seeking donations and capital funding for meaningful community impact. That makes this a practical time to talk about what you can contribute and what the Foundation needs.

Time and skills

If you would like to help, introduce yourself and describe the skills, experience, or time you could offer. Let us know whether you are interested in outreach, organizing resources, sharing the mission, or another contribution.

Contacting us starts a conversation; we will discuss a specific opportunity and next steps directly.

Supplies and community resources

Supplies and outreach are among our current funding priorities. If you have materials, space, professional skills, or community connections you would like to offer, tell us what is available and how you think it could help.

Please contact the Foundation before sending supplies or arranging a delivery. Our published address is a mailing address for correspondence.

Funding and introductions

You can also help by making a charitable gift, discussing a potential funding opportunity, or introducing the Foundation to people who may want to support its purpose.
