Using this website

This is a public information website for Sandia Purpose Foundation. It does not have visitor accounts, a contact submission form, or a checkout that collects payment details. The Foundation has not added advertising or analytics trackers to these pages.

Contacting the Foundation

Email links open your email application. Information you choose to send is received in the Foundation’s email account and used to respond to your inquiry. Keep an initial message brief and avoid including detailed medical records or other sensitive information.

Giving through PayPal Giving Fund

Donation links lead to PayPal Giving Fund. Donation and payment information is handled on that service under its own terms and privacy information. This website does not collect your card or bank details.

Website hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may receive technical request information when your browser loads the site. Read GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Privacy questions

Contact rgutierrez@sandiapurpose.com with questions about information you have provided to the Foundation.
